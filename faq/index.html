A few practical details

Frequently asked questions.

What is a personal hotel advisory?

Late Checkout helps clients choose and book exceptional hotels, access preferred benefits when available, and receive personal support before, during and after each stay.

Why use a hotel advisor instead of booking directly?

At many exceptional hotels, we can arrange the same publicly available rate with preferred recognition and additional benefits. We also work directly with the property on the details that matter to your stay. Benefits vary by hotel, rate and availability.

Does it cost anything to request access?

No. It is free to request access and join the Late Checkout client roster. We review each request personally and will follow up directly if we are able to work together.

Do you charge a fee for hotel bookings?

No. There is no fee to book a hotel through Late Checkout. We are generally compensated by the hotel after a completed stay. If a request involves work beyond the hotel booking and requires a separate planning fee, we will always discuss it with you before beginning.

Will the rate be higher?

No. In many cases, we can offer a lower rate. In others, the rate is the same as the hotel’s public rate but includes preferred benefits. Rates vary by hotel and availability, and we will always tell you if we cannot match or beat a rate you find on your own before anything is confirmed.

What benefits might be included?

Depending on the hotel and rate, benefits may include VIP recognition, daily breakfast, a hotel credit, upgrade priority, early check-in or late checkout. No benefit is guaranteed unless it appears in your booking confirmation.

Can I earn hotel points and status credit?

Often, yes, when an eligible rate is booked. We can add your loyalty number to the reservation and will flag rates that may not qualify before you approve the booking.

Where have you booked hotels?

From New York to Barcelona, Hawaii to Las Vegas, and everywhere in between. Late Checkout books exceptional hotels across the United States and around the world.

Do you arrange flights?

Late Checkout is intentionally hotel-focused. We generally recommend booking flights directly with the airline or using points and miles, while we concentrate on the hotel experience.
