A few practical details

Frequently asked questions.

What is Late Checkout Travel?

Late Checkout gives clients a better way to book exceptional hotels. At participating properties, we place eligible reservations into the hotel’s VIP or preferred-partner program, make sure the included benefits are attached, and remain available if anything comes up before or during the stay.

How does Late Checkout work?

Send us the hotel you want—or simply a destination and dates. We’ll find the strongest available rate and benefits, send you a clear option to review, and book it once you say yes. No research project or planning call required.

Why book through Late Checkout instead of directly?

Because the room may be the same, but the reservation is not. An eligible Late Checkout booking can include VIP recognition, breakfast, hotel credits, priority upgrades, and flexible check-in or checkout—often at the same rate as booking directly. We confirm what is included and work with the hotel on your behalf.

Why request client access?

Client access is free, with no subscription or obligation. Once you’re on the roster, you have a direct way to send us any hotel stay and have us handle the rate, benefits, VIP registration and booking.

Do I need to know which hotel I want?

Not at all. Send us the exact hotel if you already know it. If you don’t, tell us the destination, dates, budget and what matters to you. We’ll narrow the field and send back the strongest options.

Do you charge a fee for hotel bookings?

No. There is no fee to book a hotel through Late Checkout. We are generally compensated by the hotel after your stay. If you ask for something beyond the hotel booking that requires a separate planning fee, we’ll explain it before any work begins.

Will the rate be higher?

No. In many cases, we can offer a lower rate. In others, the rate is the same as the hotel’s public rate but includes preferred benefits. We’ll always tell you if we cannot match or beat a rate you find on your own before anything is confirmed.

What benefits might be included?

Through Fora and the Virtuoso network, Late Checkout has access to thousands of preferred hotel relationships worldwide—from Four Seasons Preferred Partner and Rosewood Elite to Raffles, Auberge Resorts Collection, Tablet Hotels and exceptional independent properties. Depending on the hotel and rate, this can unlock VIP recognition, daily breakfast, hotel or resort credits, welcome amenities, priority upgrades, and flexible check-in and checkout—often at the same rate as booking direct. We’ll find the strongest available option and confirm everything included before you book.

Can I earn hotel points and status credit?

Yes, absolutely. We add your loyalty number to the reservation so you can earn eligible hotel points and status credit and enjoy your existing status benefits. If a specific rate does not qualify, we’ll let you know before you book.

Where have you booked hotels?

From New York to Barcelona, Hawaii to Las Vegas, and everywhere in between. We book exceptional hotels across the United States and around the world.

Do you arrange flights?

Late Checkout is intentionally hotel-focused. We recommend booking flights directly with the airline or using points and miles, while we focus our attention and relationships on creating a better hotel stay.
