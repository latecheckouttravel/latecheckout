What We Heard

A beautiful setting is only the beginning.

You love the understated elegance of Blackberry Farm and High Hampton. Places with character, beautiful grounds, and a sense of place. Somewhere that feels more like a wonderful country estate than a traditional luxury resort.

Exceptional food and genuinely attentive service matter just as much as the setting. Your experience at Montage Palmetto Bluff made that clear: a beautiful property isn’t enough if the hospitality doesn’t deliver.

For this trip, you’re looking for that same feeling, with a few summer essentials: a beautiful outdoor pool, activities your six- and eight-year-old will love, and comfortable August weather.

The goal: a family vacation that feels every bit as special for the adults as it does for the kids.

The Shortlist

Five distinct ways
to get this right.

Each property answers the brief differently. The notes below focus on fit—not simply reputation.

01

Meadows of Dan, Virginia

Primland,
Auberge Collection

Primland sits on a sprawling Blue Ridge estate where the draw is the outdoors: wooded trails, stargazing, and dinner after a day away from everything. It has the secluded-country-house appeal you love, but the pool is indoors—not the sunny summer centerpiece you’re picturing.

Our judgmentStrong aesthetic match, but the indoor pool and potentially humid August weather are meaningful compromises.

02

Philipsburg, Montana

The Ranch at
Rock Creek

At this Montana ranch, campfire stories and tasting menus both belong. Horseback rides and fly-fishing fill the day; refined dinners follow. The family experiences are the main event, with the outdoor pool playing a supporting role.

Our judgmentPossibly the most compelling experiential fit, but this is a ranch vacation first and a pool vacation second.

03

Mountain Village, Colorado

Madeline Hotel
& Residences

High in Telluride’s Mountain Village, Madeline puts a heated outdoor pool and mountain views within reach of gondola rides into town. It’s a polished base for a family summer, though getting there from Chicago may require a connection.

Our judgmentA particularly strong option for summer weather and the pool. Less of a country-house experience.

04

Vail, Colorado

Four Seasons
Resort Vail

Four Seasons Vail makes the easy version of a Colorado summer appealing: a proper outdoor pool, mountain adventures nearby, and Vail Village restaurants a short stroll away. More classic luxury hotel than private country estate, but that’s part of its convenience.

Our judgmentA practical, polished choice that delivers on the pool and access to activities, although it lacks some of the intimacy you prefer.

05

Kohala Coast, Hawai‘i Island

Mauna Lani,
Auberge Collection

Mauna Lani is unmistakably Hawai‘i: black lava rock, ancient fishponds, outrigger canoe culture, and sunset light along the Kohala Coast. It’s less about Caribbean-style bathwater and more about the landscape and sense of place. The catch is the long flight from Chicago.

Our judgmentAn intriguing long-haul exception, not a direct match for your flight preference or ideal temperatures.

The Details

A closer look.

What distinguishes each stay, where it may compromise, and what we would verify before booking.

01 · Virginia

Primland

The closest country-estate atmosphere on the list.

August averages81° / 61°F
From Chicago~2h 15m flight + ~2h drive
Tripadvisor4.7 · 1,503 reviews
Closest August check~$1,000 / night

Why we like it

Twelve thousand acres, mature Blue Ridge scenery and lodge-like interiors make Primland feel removed from ordinary resort life. Recent guests consistently highlight the warmth of the staff, beautiful grounds and a relaxed version of luxury rather than ceremony for its own sake.

Visit Primland

For the kids

Hiking, biking, archery, RTV excursions, disc golf and stargazing give each day structure without making the stay feel like a children’s resort.

For the adults

Auberge spa treatments, golf, quiet trails and the observatory create genuine adult time around the family activities.

Food & service

Recent August guests praise both the dining and unusually thoughtful service. Condé Nast Traveler emphasizes the estate’s outdoor pursuits and secluded Blue Ridge setting.

Included & available

Fitness facilities, hiking and selected resort activities are available; guided adventures, spa and some outdoor pursuits carry separate charges. Exact inclusions depend on the rate.

Considerations

The main lodge pool is indoors. August is mild by Southern standards but warmer and more humid than Colorado or Montana, and the ground transfer is substantial.

Rate context

Early-August 2027 availability was approximately $1,000 nightly when checked. Mid-August was not showing online; we would confirm the closest available dates, family room category, taxes and terms directly with the resort.

02 · Montana

The Ranch at Rock Creek

The strongest experiential answer to the Blackberry Farm brief.

August averages81° / 42°F
From Chicago~4h flight + 1h 30m transfer
Tripadvisor4.9 · 722 reviews
Planning estimateFrom ~$3,200, all-inclusive

Why we like it

This is the option where the days themselves become the luxury. Riding, fishing, archery and family adventures are matched by polished meals and deeply personal service. The Ranch feels special because of what happens there, not because it announces its price tag.

Visit The Ranch

For the kids

Horseback riding, fly-fishing, archery, hiking, biking and a broad menu of guided activities. Recent multigenerational families report that the team personalizes days exceptionally well.

For the adults

Serious outdoor experiences, spa time, wine and refined ranch dining—without needing to coordinate each element separately.

Food & service

Recent Tripadvisor reviews are unusually consistent on both. Condé Nast Traveler and a 2026 Daily Beast review also highlight the combination of chef-led dining and activity personalization.

What’s included

Lodging, meals, selected ranch activities, equipment and transfers from listed Montana airports are generally part of the all-inclusive model. Spa and certain premium experiences may be additional.

Considerations

The outdoor pool is pleasant but not the reason to come. The price is materially higher than the other domestic options, though the inclusive structure narrows the true cost difference.

Rate context

Current market evidence starts around $3,200 nightly and rises sharply by accommodation. Mid-August 2027 pricing is not publicly confirmed; we would request a family quote because meals, transfers and many activities are bundled.

03 · Colorado

Madeline Hotel & Residences

The best blend of pool, cool summer weather and residential comfort.

August averages74° / 40°F
From ChicagoConnection likely + ~1h 30m transfer
Tripadvisor4.6 · 398 reviews
Planning estimate$700–$1,500 / night

Why we like it

Madeline answers the summer brief cleanly: a heated outdoor pool looking toward the San Juan Mountains, residences that feel like a real home base, and a complimentary gondola connecting Mountain Village with Telluride.

Visit Madeline

For the kids

The 20 Below program, pool, gondola rides, hiking, summer concerts, movies under the stars and family mountain adventures. Program dates should be confirmed for mid-August.

For the adults

Auberge spa, mountain dining, easy evenings in Telluride and the option of a residence with space to spread out.

Food & service

Recent summer reviews praise the concierge, pool, food and overall service. Condé Nast Traveler notes the personalized service and slope-side setting, though its dining coverage may predate current concepts.

Included & available

The $50 nightly resort fee currently covers fitness classes, welcome drinks, coffee and water, sunset s’mores, music, summer movies, maps and selected programming.

Considerations

Chicago’s nonstop to Montrose is not yet confirmed for August 2027, so a connection may be required. This is a mountain hotel rather than a secluded estate.

Rate context

Plan on roughly $700–$1,500 nightly until mid-August 2027 inventory is verified. Auberge currently publishes savings of up to 30% for stays of six nights or more, subject to dates and availability.

04 · Colorado

Four Seasons Resort Vail

The easiest polished mountain-resort choice.

August averages74° / 41°F
From Chicago~3h 15m flight + 30–45m transfer
Tripadvisor4.6 · 1,509 reviews
Planning estimate$800–$1,600 / night

Why we like it

Four Seasons Vail removes friction. The pool is a real part of the day, the village is walkable, and the concierge can make the surrounding mountains feel effortless. It is less intimate than Sarah’s ideal—but probably the simplest trip to execute beautifully.

Visit Four Seasons Vail

For the kids

Heated outdoor pool, summer hiking, horseback riding, rafting and Adventure Ridge. Kids For All Seasons and babysitting are available seasonally and should be confirmed for the stay.

For the adults

Spa, Vail Village restaurants, trail access and a true pool day without sacrificing mountain scenery.

Food & service

Condé Nast Traveler’s hotel review and 2026 return-guest essay both support the service proposition. The hotel offers poolside dining, The Remedy and easy access to town.

Included & available

Pool amenities, fitness access, twice-daily housekeeping, morning coffee, village shuttle and selected family touches are complimentary. Meals, adventures and spa are additional.

Considerations

It feels like a polished luxury hotel rather than a country estate. The pool may use a waitlist and time limit when capacity is reached.

Rate context

Plan on roughly $800–$1,600 nightly until exact family inventory is verified. The hotel’s fourth-night-free offer includes selected dates through 2027 and could materially improve an eight-night stay.

05 · Hawai‘i

Mauna Lani

The strongest complete resort experience—and the clear flight exception.

August averages87° / 74°F
From Chicago~9h if nonstop + 25–30m transfer
Tripadvisor4.7 · 1,589 reviews
Planning estimate$900–$1,600 / night

Why we like it

Mauna Lani has the rare combination of a beautiful setting, real cultural identity, excellent family infrastructure and food adults can look forward to. It is the only option here that earns top fit marks in every experiential category.

Visit Mauna Lani

For the kids

Complimentary Holoholo Kids Crew for ages 5–12, a sand-bottom keiki pool, family pool, snorkeling, paddleboarding, bikes and culturally grounded activities.

For the adults

An adults’ pool, Auberge spa, CanoeHouse, ocean pursuits, tennis, golf and one of the Kohala Coast’s strongest senses of place.

Food & service

Recent families praise pool and beach service and CanoeHouse. A 2026 Condé Nast Traveler essay centers the property’s repeat appeal; Travel + Leisure readers ranked it among Hawai‘i’s leading resorts in 2025.

What’s included

The current $60 nightly resort fee includes Holoholo activities, snorkeling gear, paddleboards, bikes, tennis and pickleball, yoga, cultural programming and local shuttle service.

Considerations

The flight is far beyond the preferred four hours, and August is warmer than the mountain options. The 2027 Chicago nonstop schedule is not yet confirmed.

Rate context

Plan on roughly $900–$1,600 nightly for a standard room until mid-August 2027 inventory is verified. Suites and residences cost more; length-of-stay or resort-credit offers may improve the final value.
