What We Heard

A beautiful setting is only the beginning.

You love the understated elegance of Blackberry Farm and High Hampton. Places with character, beautiful grounds, and a sense of place. Somewhere that feels more like a wonderful country estate than a traditional luxury resort.

Exceptional food and genuinely attentive service matter just as much as the setting. Your experience at Montage Palmetto Bluff made that clear: a beautiful property isn’t enough if the hospitality doesn’t deliver.

For this trip, you’re looking for that same feeling, with a few summer essentials: a beautiful outdoor pool, activities your six- and eight-year-old will love, and comfortable August weather.

The goal: a family vacation that feels every bit as special for the adults as it does for the kids.

Why we like it

Twelve thousand acres, mature Blue Ridge scenery and lodge-like interiors make Primland feel removed from ordinary resort life. Recent guests consistently highlight the warmth of the staff, beautiful grounds and a relaxed version of luxury rather than ceremony for its own sake.

Visit Primland
A note from Sara

I haven’t stayed at Primland myself, but I gave a weekend there to a dear friend in Richmond for her birthday. She had an incredible time. I’ve also worked directly with the property’s reservations team and found them exceptionally responsive and helpful.

For the kids

Junior Mountaineers Kids Club gives children ages 6–12 half- or full-day nature programs with activities such as pond fishing, foraging, archery and horseback riding. Primland also lists Saturday Kids Nights for ages 3–10 in July and August 2027—particularly useful for Sarah’s six- and eight-year-old.

For the adults

Auberge spa treatments, golf, quiet trails and the observatory create genuine adult time around the family activities.

Fora advisor & client reviews

Across 14 advisor reviews and 27 client reviews, the pattern is unusually consistent: staff remember names and preferences, the estate feels peaceful even when full, and stargazing is repeatedly singled out as a reason to come. Guests also praise the Blue Ridge views and the ease of moving from the lodge into activities. The recurring cautions are the winding arrival, occasional shuttle delays, activity costs and dining menus that can feel limited during a week-long stay. Condé Nast Traveler similarly emphasizes the secluded setting and outdoor pursuits.

Included & available

Fitness facilities, hiking and selected resort activities are available; guided adventures, spa and some outdoor pursuits carry separate charges. Exact inclusions depend on the rate.

Considerations

The current main lodge pool is indoors. A Fora advisor who visited in July 2026 reported that a new outdoor infinity pool and pool house were targeting summer 2027, but the opening date should be confirmed before this trip. August is warmer and more humid than Colorado, and the final approach to the lodge is long and winding.

Rate context

Early-August 2027 availability was approximately $1,000 nightly when checked. Mid-August was not showing online; we would confirm the closest available dates, family room category, taxes and terms directly with the resort.

Why we like it

Nemacolin directly answers the option Sarah had already been considering. Across 2,200 acres, it combines a genuine summer pool scene, an unusually deep activity roster and destination-level dining. The Grand Lodge is the accommodation we would investigate first for its large suites and butler service.

Visit Nemacolin

For the kids

Four family-friendly outdoor pools, a waterslide, kids’ club, ropes course, pony encounters, wildlife experiences, mini golf, climbing, art and summer programming make this the broadest children’s offering on the list.

For the adults

A serious spa, two Pete Dye golf courses, art, wine experiences, Fallingwater nearby and enough dining range for a longer stay. Babysitting and supervised programming create real adult time.

Food & service

Lautrec holds Forbes Five-Star recognition, with Fawn & Fable and many more relaxed family options across the resort. Recent guests praise the Grand Lodge, pools and individual service; Condé Nast Traveler finds the hospitality attentive, if occasionally scattered. Travel + Leisure highlights the Grand Lodge’s all-suite format, butler service and woodsy, whimsical design.

Included & available

Pool access and resort transportation are available to overnight guests. Many signature activities, dining, spa and cabanas cost extra and should be reserved early; the concierge can build the days around the family’s priorities.

Considerations

Nemacolin is deliberately grand, whimsical and sprawling—not the quiet, understated country-estate atmosphere Sarah loved at Blackberry Farm. The activity logistics require planning, and some recent families report inconsistent coordination despite otherwise strong service.

Rate context

Current public pricing suggests roughly $830–$1,500+ nightly before taxes and fees, with Grand Lodge family suites potentially higher. Mid-August 2027 inventory and the best two-bed or connecting configuration still need to be priced directly.
