Why book through a travel advisor?

Why should I use you instead of booking the hotel myself?

Because for no extra cost, you can get preferential treatment. At many luxury hotels, I can access preferred advisor programs that may include breakfast, hotel credits, room upgrades, early check-in, late checkout, and often a better rate. Same hotel. Better access. Better treatment.

Is there a fee to book through you?

No—there is no booking fee to use me. I’m compensated directly by the hotel, while you get the benefit of preferred rates, added perks, and eligible loyalty credit. I get paid by the hotel. You get a better stay and save money. Win-win.

Are your rates higher than booking direct?

Usually, no. In most cases, I can book the same publicly available rate with added benefits, and often have access to preferred or promotional rates as well. I always compare against the hotel’s direct rate and show you the best options before you book.

What kinds of perks can you get?

Depending on the hotel and rate, benefits can include daily breakfast, hotel or resort credits, room upgrades, early check-in, late checkout, welcome amenities, and preferred rates. Not every hotel offers every benefit, and upgrades and timing requests are often subject to availability.

How it works

How do I book a hotel with you?

Use the Book a Hotel form and send me your destination, dates, and anything you’re already considering. Know exactly where you want to stay? Great. Choosing between a few hotels? Send those too. Have no idea yet? Also fine. No call required.

What happens after I send the form?

I’ll review the hotel options, rates, and any preferred-partner benefits available. If you need help choosing, I’ll narrow down the options. If you already know what you want, we can go straight to the booking. You approve the hotel and rate before it’s confirmed.

How quickly will I hear back?

Most hotel requests are reviewed within 24–48 hours. If your trip is sooner, send it anyway. I’ll do my best to move quickly.

Can you help me choose the hotel, or do I need to know what I want already?

Both. Some people send me one hotel and dates. Others send three hotels and ask, “Which one?” Helping you figure out which hotel actually fits the trip is part of the fun.

Points, miles & loyalty

Should I use points for flights or hotels?

My general rule is to use points and miles where they give you great value, often on flights, and let me check the hotel. A cash hotel booking through an advisor can sometimes come with meaningful perks while still earning hotel loyalty credit when the rate is eligible. There are always exceptions, but it’s a good place to start.

Do you book flights?

Late Checkout is hotel-focused. For flights, I generally prefer booking directly with the airline or using points and miles, especially when that gives you more control over changes and your loyalty benefits. Then I can focus on making the hotel side of the trip better.

Can I still earn hotel points and status when I book through you?

Often, yes. For eligible hotel rates, I can add your loyalty number to the reservation so you can earn points and receive any applicable status benefits. Some special rates may not qualify, and I’ll flag that before you book.

Can I use hotel points and still get advisor perks?

Usually not on the same stay. Award bookings and advisor-preferred rates are generally different booking channels. If you’re deciding whether to use points or pay cash, send me the dates and I can help you compare the options.

A few other things

What if I already booked the hotel?

If your reservation is refundable, send it over. I can check whether there’s a better rate or a way to book the same stay with more included. Don’t cancel anything until a replacement reservation is confirmed.

Do you only book luxury hotels?

Late Checkout is focused on great hotels, particularly independent, boutique, and luxury properties where advisor relationships and preferred programs can make a meaningful difference. That doesn’t mean every stay needs to be wildly expensive. The right hotel is the one that makes sense for the trip.

Can you help with more than one hotel?

Yes. If you’re taking a trip with multiple stops, send me the itinerary and I can help with the hotels along the way.

What if something goes wrong during my stay?

Reach out. One of the advantages of booking through an advisor is having someone in your corner who can contact the hotel and help when something needs attention.
