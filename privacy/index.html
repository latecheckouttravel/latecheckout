Privacy Policy

Effective September 25, 2026

This policy explains how Late Checkout Travel (“Late Checkout,” “we,” “us”) handles information collected through latecheckouttravel.com. Late Checkout is an independent hotel advisory affiliated with Fora Travel, Inc.

Information we collect

When you request access, we collect the information you provide: your first and last name, email address, typical nightly hotel budget, what you would like help with, referral source and the page from which you submitted the request. We also use Google Analytics, which may collect device, browser, approximate location and website-use information through cookies and similar technologies.

How we use information

We use request information to review your interest in becoming a client, communicate with you, prevent spam and operate our advisory. Submitting a request does not create a client relationship or guarantee a response or invitation.

How information is shared

We use service providers where needed to operate this website, deliver request emails and understand website performance. If you are invited to become a client or make a travel booking, relevant information may be shared with Fora Travel, hotels and other travel providers as needed to respond to your inquiry or provide requested travel services.

Late Checkout does not sell personal information. We may disclose information when required by law, to protect rights or safety, or in connection with a business transfer.

Fora Travel

Travel inquiries and bookings facilitated through the Fora Travel network may also be governed by Fora Travel’s Privacy Policy. Fora and Late Checkout may act independently with respect to information each collects.

Retention and security

We retain personal information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above and applicable legal obligations. We use reasonable safeguards, but no internet transmission or storage system is completely secure.

Your choices

You may ask to access, correct or delete personal information held by Late Checkout by emailing hello@latecheckouttravel.com. You may control cookies through your browser settings.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

We may update this policy as our services or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the current version.

Questions about Late Checkout’s privacy practices may be sent to hello@latecheckouttravel.com. Questions about information held by Fora should be directed to privacy@foratravel.com.
