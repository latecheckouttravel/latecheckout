About Stay

Stay is a registered travel advisor operating under a valid IATA accreditation.

We review and manage luxury hotel bookings, typically $800+ per night, and provide access to preferred partner rates and amenities through our global network of more than 8,200 hotels and travel providers.

Our partnerships include Virtuoso, Four Seasons, Rosewood, Belmond, and other leading luxury brands worldwide. Through these relationships, eligible bookings may include:

  • Room upgrades (when available)
  • Complimentary daily breakfast
  • Resort or property credits
  • Early check-in and late check-out (when available)
  • Priority status and on-property recognition

When you book through Stay - or when we rebook an eligible reservation - you benefit from these partnerships at no additional cost to you.

Stay is compensated through commissions paid by hotels and travel providers. Our clients do not pay service fees for standard hotel bookings.

If we review an existing reservation and identify a lower refundable rate or a more advantageous booking channel, we present the option to you before any change is made. Nothing is modified without your approval.

Stay helps you get the most from your hotel booking - without paying more for it.
