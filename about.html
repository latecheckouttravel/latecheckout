Hi, I’m Sara.

I’m a San Francisco-based travel advisor and the person behind Late Checkout.

I spend a lot of time thinking about hotels, where to go next, and the little things that make a trip feel special.

How I like
to travel

I tend to prefer seeing a little less and staying a little longer. A tucked-away café over a checklist. A hotel with a real sense of place over one that could be anywhere. Luxury that shows up in the details rather than announcing itself.

What I do

I help travelers find and book great hotels around the world.

Sometimes you already know exactly where you want to stay. Sometimes you’re deciding between three hotels and want someone who actually knows the difference. Either works.

As a travel advisor with Fora, I have access to preferred hotel programs that can include complimentary breakfast, hotel credits, upgrades, early check-in, late checkout, and preferred rates.

And I keep the process easy. Send me where you’re going, your dates, and anything you’re already considering. No call required.
