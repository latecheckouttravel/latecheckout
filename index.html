Relationships Matter

Booking is easy.
Relationships are different.

Technology can help you find a hotel and make a reservation.

What it can’t replace is the relationship with the property: knowing who to contact, communicating what matters, advocating for your stay, and stepping in when something changes.

Founder

Meet the Founder.

Sara is a San Francisco-based product executive, travel advisor and lifelong hotel obsessive.

She founded Late Checkout around a simple idea: travel is easier when someone knows you, and better when the hotel knows you’re coming.

Late Checkout is an independent travel advisory within the Fora Travel network, giving clients access to preferred hotel partnerships and benefits around the world.
