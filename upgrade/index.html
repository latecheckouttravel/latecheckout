Can a travel advisor improve an existing hotel reservation?

If your luxury hotel reservation is still refundable, Late Checkout Travel can review the same hotel, room, and dates before the cancellation deadline. We look for a lower refundable rate or a preferred partner booking that includes more value.

What we check

We compare the details of your current reservation with eligible rates across our travel partner network. Nothing is canceled or rebooked without your approval.

Benefits we may find

Depending on the hotel, benefits may include daily breakfast, a resort or property credit, room upgrade eligibility, early check-in, late check-out, and VIP recognition.

What it costs

There is no service fee for a standard hotel reservation review. Hotels and travel providers compensate Late Checkout Travel when an eligible booking is completed.
